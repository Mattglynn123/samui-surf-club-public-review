Partner With Our Club

Club sponsorship supports free ocean-safety programs for local children, essential rescue and first-aid equipment, volunteer training, uniforms, school visits and the development of a permanent beach clubhouse.

Your support helps children build confidence in the ocean, learn practical lifesaving skills and work toward recognised qualifications and future career pathways.
