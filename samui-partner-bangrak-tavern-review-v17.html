Partnership

Bangrak Tavern and Koh Samui Surf Lifesaving Club

Bangrak Tavern and its owner, Greg, have been great supporters of the Koh Samui Surf Lifesaving Club. Working in partnership with the club, Bangrak Tavern helped establish a pool competition between the tavern and the surf club, creating another great opportunity to bring the two communities together.

Greg and Bangrak Tavern have also gone above and beyond in supporting the club's operations. When our speaker required repairs, they provided a replacement service free of charge and kindly loaned the club another speaker while ours was being repaired. The Tavern also promotes the Surf Club on its bar TVs, and their generosity and ongoing support have been greatly appreciated by the club.

Koh Samui Surf Lifesaving Club is a community-led volunteer club building safer beaches, stronger youth programs and practical lifesaving skills on Koh Samui.
