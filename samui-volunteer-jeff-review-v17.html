Jeff

Jeff was one of the original members present at the inaugural Koh Samui Surf Lifesaving Club meeting in December 2025 and became a regular and valued supporter during the club's early days.

Every Sunday morning, Jeff would come down to the beach to help the children with their ocean swimming and assist with keeping a watchful eye over the kids from the surf rescue paddleboard. His presence in the water provided an important extra level of safety while the children developed their confidence and ocean skills.

Originally from America and a long-term resident of Thailand, Jeff has embraced the local community and has been a fantastic supporter of the club from its earliest days.

We are very grateful to Jeff for giving his time, getting involved in the water and helping the club establish a strong foundation in those first months.
